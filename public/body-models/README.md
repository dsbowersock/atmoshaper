# Wellness body visualization

`human-base-rigged.glb` is the unmodified, self-contained Innerscene human base
mesh with editable 53-bone rig (MakeHuman/MPFB-derived), released CC0.

- Author/source: https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/human-base-mesh-with-editable-53-bone-rig-8e7c8ab1
- Download: https://files.innerscene.com/library/human-base-mesh-with-editable-53-bone-rig-8e7c8ab1/329f39cef2f95a0f0a24/human-base-rigged.glb
- Author terms: https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/terms
- Upstream asset license: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/master/LICENSE.ASSETS.md
- SHA-256: `f3cd655e44af0a79786b1b4349c4acf1ae342fe6bd0d5fc714e68470dea1b429`
- Bytes: 4,994,820; glTF 2.0; one skinned mesh; no external resources/textures.

Embedded metadata also states Innerscene's CC0 dedication. There is no license
fee or required attribution. `CC0.txt` retains the upstream dedication text.
Application lighting/material and illustrative bone poses are authored here.
The rig has separate finger/thumb bones and one collective toe bone per foot.
It cannot demonstrate isolated toes or toe spreading. Those selectable movements
use written guidance and an explicit unavailable-animation message; no isolated
toe motion is inferred from moving the whole foot.
This is a surface illustration, not a muscle atlas, measurement instrument or
validated biomechanical simulation. No normal-range or treatment claims are
derived from its proportions, rig or animation. No third-party runtime service
is used. `npm run body-model:inspect` verifies the local geometry and bone map.
