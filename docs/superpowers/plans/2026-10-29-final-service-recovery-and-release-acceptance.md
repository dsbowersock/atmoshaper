### Acceptance sequence

1. **Pre-build preparation:** establish reviewed source, provider/catalog/tax/legal/rights/configuration prerequisites and legitimately supported readiness attestations for the proposed environment. Never set `BACKGROUND_COMMERCE_WEBHOOK_READY` or another missing readiness field true merely to pass a build. If a required proof cannot exist yet, prepare the explicit staged/provisional acceptance proposal rather than bypass a gate or imply that no candidate may ever be built.
2. **Candidate-only enablement:** obtain separate authority for an isolated/staged all-payments candidate, its build, configuration and exact acceptance operations. Enable the new purchase flags only in that approved candidate scope and preserve existing public routing. The proposal must first identify a feasible environment, protection and webhook path; this plan assumes no provider Preview technique, shared configuration isolation or new permission.
3. **Public-context acceptance when necessary:** if a particular observation needs the public context, prepare a separately approved bounded provisional promotion/activation with exact hosts, enabled flows, fixture/payment/provider/DB access, request/time/cost limits, monitoring, all-flow containment, saved artifact/configuration/alias rollback and verification. Its containment must cover recurring Supporter, donation and background purchases plus in-flight webhook/reconciliation effects. The existing registration/Supporter pauses do not provide that global containment. No provisional action is inferred from an old merge, build or activation receipt, and none is authorized now.
4. **Final acceptance and public/marketing activation:** complete the actual missing all-payments configuration/webhook/browser and reconciliation proof, then obtain the final release decision. The two new flows stay disabled on the existing public service except within an explicitly approved bounded provisional scope; candidate-only enablement is not general public activation. A Supporter-only READY marker is not all-payments acceptance. Media cleanup/integrity still does not close rights, branded delivery/CORS/cache/range/playback or catalog binding/write-backout.
