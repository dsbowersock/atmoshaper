# Individual accounts and practice/group workspaces

Status: owner-provided product direction, recorded November 20, 2026. This is not
an approved billing migration or a change to existing permissions/subscriptions.

The owner supplied their earlier note: each person creates an individual account
and subscribes. People can create a practice/group workspace or be invited to
someone else's, and can belong to multiple practices, businesses or groups.
Practice scheduling could be a monthly addition paid by one person or divided
among consenting invited members. The owner has not settled whether that is one
individual subscriber level plus an add-on, or another subscription level.

- The individual account remains the durable identity. A person may own and join
  multiple workspaces; group membership does not replace their account.
- Personal self-tracking and calendars belong to the individual. Joining a
  workspace does not grant it access to that person's private records.
- Practice scheduling follows workspace-specific authorization. Membership in
  one workspace does not grant access to another workspace's information.
- A combined calendar is a private display of sources the viewer may already
  read. It does not merge storage, create sharing grants or copy records.
- Pricing, sponsoring, split-payment consent, departures, record ownership and
  migration of existing subscriptions need a separate concrete design. An
  invitation cannot silently charge an invitee.

The sidebar/calendar branch preserves current subscriptions and the existing
many-practice membership model. No provider, schema, membership, invitation or
sharing change is included in that branch.
