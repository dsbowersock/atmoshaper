# Small beta tester guide

Welcome to AtmoShaper's small beta. Try the tools you would actually use and
report problems or confusing steps using the contact details below.

## Getting started

Open [AtmoShaper](https://www.atmoshaper.com/) in your usual browser. Keep note
of whether you are on a phone, tablet or computer. Use your own account for
sign-in and settings checks. If a step fails, record it before trying again.

For practice and professional-record tests, use invented people and appointments.
Local professional records stay in that browser/device. The site's shared
database backups do not back up those local records; use the tool's own export
and recovery workflow for any local test material you want to keep.

## Suggested checks

- **Account:** sign in, open settings, sign out and return. Report confusing
  instructions or a setting that does not persist.
- **Clock and Chimer:** try a short session, start/pause/reset, sound controls
  and the views you would use in a treatment room. Check on your actual device.
- **Atmosphere:** choose an offered sound or station, adjust its volume, pause
  and resume. Report the exact selection if audio fails or keeps loading.
- **Anatomime and education:** try the available study/game flows and report
  navigation problems, unclear instructions or visibly incorrect content.
- **Calendar:** use an invented practice/appointment. Google connection is
  optional. Review its consent yourself: it lists your calendars, reads busy
  times and manages events in a dedicated app-created calendar. Use your own
  account and do not invite real clients as part of this beta test.
- **Phone and desktop:** check readable text, menus, keyboard access and any
  offered install/offline behavior. Describe what remains usable when offline.

Do not make a purchase solely to test a payment path. Payment acceptance has its
own owner-coordinated test scope; available account features may depend on access.

## Reporting a problem

Use the site's [support page](https://www.atmoshaper.com/support) or its current
public support address, `atmoshaper@gmail.com`. Derrick monitors this inbox for
beta feedback. The support form opens your email app with a draft; send the
message there, or compose an email directly. Include:

1. The page/tool and steps that reproduce the problem.
2. What you expected and what happened.
3. Approximate time, device, browser and whether it happens again.
4. A screenshot with personal/client details removed, and a diagnostic reference
   if the app offers one.

Keep passwords, codes, keys, client details, clinical records, full database
exports and raw private logs out of feedback. Reports should describe the
problem without sharing the underlying sensitive data.
