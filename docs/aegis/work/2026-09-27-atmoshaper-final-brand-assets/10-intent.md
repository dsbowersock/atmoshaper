# AtmoShaper final brand-assets intent

## Intent lock

Integrate the user-approved AtmoShaper logo, logo-plus-name wordmark, integrated
word treatment, favicon, PWA icons, and Apple icon into the application without
modifying or deleting the original export library.

## Scope fence

Allowed:

- Copy selected runtime-size exports into stable tracked asset paths.
- Update public presentation identity and intrinsic image dimensions.
- Replace favicon, PWA, and Apple icon bytes.
- Validate responsive rendering and publish an unmerged focused PR.

Not allowed:

- Regenerate, recolor, crop, or overwrite the user's originals.
- Commit oversized master/source variants.
- Provider, deployment, domain, OAuth, Stripe, or database mutation.
- Merge without fresh authorization.
