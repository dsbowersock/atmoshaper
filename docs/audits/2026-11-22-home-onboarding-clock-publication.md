returning-user dashboard. Its header uses the approved wordmark and Practice
tools uses the orange material. All tools and focused study/practice sheets
retain their separate Calendar destinations and keyboard focus behavior.

and verification gates. Not now leaves independent Account controls available.

declining ends automatic reminders while preserving manual re-entry. Save/leave
and Finish wait for acknowledgement. Owner locking, revisions and dedicated
setup writers prevent stale sync from replaying answers. An abandoned form
cannot navigate after either a pending autosave or completion returns.

digit free to fill the enlarged display. DSEG7 Modern Bold numbers and the same
author's unmodified DSEG14 Modern Bold companion provide the reviewed lettering.
Seconds, 24-hour mode, selected fallback fonts and sizing remain supported.
