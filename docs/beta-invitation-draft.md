# Small beta invitation draft

Prepared for the owner to copy and send. Nothing has been sent by the agent.

## Copyable invitation

I'm inviting a few people to try AtmoShaper and help me find problems or confusing
steps before I share it more widely.

Start here: https://www.atmoshaper.com/

Create your own account and try one or two tools you would actually use, such as
the Clock, Chimer, Atmosphere audio, education or games. Start with a short session
on your usual phone or computer. Google Calendar connection is optional; review
its consent yourself if you choose to connect it.

Please use invented people and appointments for any practice or record tests.
Keep real client and clinical information out of this beta. You don't need to buy
anything to help test it; tell me if access or pricing is confusing.

Send feedback to atmoshaper@gmail.com. Include the page, steps, what you expected,
what happened, approximate time, and your device/browser. Screenshots help when
personal details are removed. Please don't send passwords, codes or client data.

## Owner's first round

Start with a few people using different ordinary devices. Share the
[tester guide](beta-tester-guide.md) alongside this invitation if useful.

For each tester, request only a short first session:

1. Create their own account, review the site's terms and reach the first-run
   screen. Note which sign-in method they chose and any confusing instructions.
2. Open account/settings, change an ordinary display preference if they want,
   reload and check it persists. Sign out and return using the same method.
3. Try a short timer/audio/study session. Report a stuck loading state, control
   failure or confusing access message with the exact page/selection.
4. Send a private-data-free report through their email app or directly to the
   branded support inbox. The site's support form prepares an email draft.

This is a plan for actual beta feedback, not a claim that a new live account has
already completed these steps. Existing signed-in, payment, Calendar and game
acceptance is carried forward at its recorded scope; no new paid check is needed.
